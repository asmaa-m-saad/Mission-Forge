# Mission Forge — Starbound Edition

An interactive space mission design game built for the **NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 (Cairo)**, challenge: **Space Mission Design Game**.
Our team, **Planet Her**, won **2nd place** in collaboration with **Loc & Play**.

**Play it:** https://asmaa23011194.github.io/mission-forge/

## About
Designing a space mission means balancing competing demands: objectives, spacecraft design, instruments, launch vehicles, budget, power, mass, and orbital constraints. Mission Forge lets players make those decisions themselves and see how each choice affects the outcome of their mission.

## How to play
1. Study your scenario. Different targets have different challenges.
2. Choose your launch vehicle, propulsion, solar panels, batteries, and science instruments.
3. Balance delta-v, mass, power, budget, and reliability.
4. Launch the mission and see your score.

Missions include Mars climate and atmosphere, Mars subsurface water, lunar polar resources, Europa exploration, and asteroid characterisation. Aliuna, our in-game guide, introduces the basics.

## Run locally
No build step. Download the repo and open `index.html` in a browser. An internet connection is needed to load Three.js and the fonts from a CDN.

## Tech
HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Three.js (3D orbit view).
